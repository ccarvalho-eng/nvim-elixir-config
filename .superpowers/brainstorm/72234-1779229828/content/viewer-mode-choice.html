WASD POV

First-person walkthrough

Feels closest to standing inside the house. Best for your “mostly to have a POV” goal.

orbit

Orbit/dollhouse view

Best for understanding room layout from above. Easier controls, less immersive.

My recommendation

  • Start with first-person walkthrough plus a small top-down minimap.
  • Add orbit view as a secondary toggle once the core model generation works.

Keep out of MVP

  • Photoreal rendering, furniture libraries, lighting simulation, and CAD export.
  • Multiple floors until one-floor plans feel solid.

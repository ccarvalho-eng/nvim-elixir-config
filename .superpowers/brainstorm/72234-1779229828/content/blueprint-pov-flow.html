Blueprint to 3D POV: practical first version

The goal is a fast spatial preview, not CAD precision. Pick the workflow that feels closest to what you want.

Guided Trace

Upload blueprint, set scale, trace walls/rooms with snapping, then generate a simple walkable 3D shell.

Auto Detect + Edit

The app guesses walls/doors from the blueprint, then the user fixes mistakes before generating the POV model.

Room Builder

Use the blueprint as a reference image, draw rough rooms on top, and generate a colored massing model quickly.

Recommended for MVP

  • Guided Trace gives the best balance of speed and reliable results.
  • It supports real uploaded blueprints without needing perfect AI parsing.
  • The 3D output can still feel interactive: orbit, first-person walk, wall height, doors, windows, floor material.

Defer initially

  • Precise structural details, roof engineering, electrical/plumbing symbols, construction documents.
  • Fully automatic interpretation of every blueprint style.
  • CAD-grade measurements and compliance checks.

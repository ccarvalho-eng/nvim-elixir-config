Image-only generation

Fastest to demo, but the results are less trustworthy because they are visual guesses rather than a model.

Recommended V1

  • Upload blueprint, detect rough walls/rooms, generate a simple 3D massing model.
  • Show preset camera views: roof-off, exterior, front, side, and maybe one interior POV.
  • Keep correction minimal: accept result or adjust obvious wall lines.

Important tradeoff

  • Without any correction step, blueprint parsing can fail on messy scans.
  • With image-only AI generation, the output may look nice but not match the plan reliably.
